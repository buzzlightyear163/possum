Field notes

Playing dead is real.

Biologists call it thanatosis. In opossums it isn't a trick or a plan. It's a stress response the body runs on its own.

Didelphis virginiana · the Virginia opossum

SPECIMEN NOTE 01

Involuntary

A scared possum doesn't choose to collapse. It goes stiff, drops, and stays down until the danger passes.

Holder diagnosis: identical.

SPECIMEN NOTE 02

Minutes to hours
